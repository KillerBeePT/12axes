# Portuguese personalities: research provenance

These research dossiers and correction notes support twelve independently simulated personality audits. Research cut-off: 2026-11-01. Portuguese originals are retained to make the sources and changes reviewable.

The personalities did not answer the questionnaire themselves. Direct statements, party programmes, institutional actions and inferred answers must be distinguished. Missing public statements about hypothetical questions do not establish neutrality. The audit reports in `../../reports/portugal/` record profile-specific limitations and sources.

The correction notes `segunda-revisao-*.md` supersede the corresponding claims in the original dossiers. Earlier chat estimates of research completeness were upper bounds from non-exhaustive lists of gaps; they are not measured confidence scores and are not used to compute the vectors.

Use the archived 240 answers and archetype choices in `../../answers/personality/`, and `profile_vector.py`, to reproduce each vector. Do not replace answers by manually selecting axis values.
